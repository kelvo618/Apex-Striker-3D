How to Play

7-a-side arcade football. You control the highlighted player (gold ring).
Keyboard: WASD/Arrows move, Shift sprint, Space tackle, J pass, K shoot (hold for power), L through ball, E switch player.
Touch: joystick on the left, buttons on the right. Hold SHOOT to charge.
Out of play restarts and fouls are automatic. Tap Kick Off in a restart to take it yourself.
