Privacy Policy

Apex Striker 3D does not collect, store or share any personal information on any server.

The game saves your settings, coins, career and tournament progress only in your own browser or device storage (localStorage). Clearing your browser or app data removes it.

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It loads the Three.js library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

All teams, players and crests are fictional.
